Our platform connects donors with NGOs to reduce food waste and help those in need. Together, we can make a difference.

👤 For Users

✔ Register and create your account
✔ Browse NGOs working in different areas
✔ Donate food or money to support NGO activities
✔ Help distribute food to people in need

🏢 For NGOs

✔ Register your NGO with valid documents
✔ Submit government certificate and PAN card
✔ Wait for admin approval
✔ Once approved, start receiving donations

⚙️ Approval Process

✔ NGOs must be verified by Admin
✔ Admin reviews documents and details
✔ Only approved NGOs are visible to users
